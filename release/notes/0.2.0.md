Skate 3's skating in Night City. Skate's own engine runs beside Cyberpunk 2077: Flick-It controls, board and skater physics, tricks, grinds, manuals, bails and session markers on Night City's streets, rails and stairs, with V riding in third person.

Deckrunner is a fork of [SK8V](https://github.com/becaring/SK8V) by becaring, built with Claude Code by a software engineer (see the README). It's an unofficial fan project, unaffiliated with EA or CD PROJEKT RED, and contains no Skate 3 or Cyberpunk 2077 code or data: a one-time setup builds everything on your PC from your own Skate 3 disc and your own game install.

## Changes in 0.2.0

- **Instant start.** Skate now loads in the background a few seconds after a save loads, so Ctrl+Shift+F10 starts skating at once instead of after about 10 seconds. If loading a save stutters on your PC, set `preload=0` under `[skate]` in `deckrunner.ini` to load Skate on the first start instead.
- **You start on foot, holding the board.** Press **Y** (Triangle) to get on. The skater no longer rolls forward on its own until you touch the controller.
- **Difficulty.** Set `difficulty=` under `[skate]` in `deckrunner.ini`: `easy` (the default, as before), `normal`, `hardcore` or `motorized`. These are Skate 3's own handling modes. It applies the next time you start skating.
- **A Skate 3 HUD, in Cyberpunk's style** (optional: needs [Codeware](https://github.com/psiberx/cp2077-codeware/releases), which needs [redscript](https://github.com/jac3km4/redscript/releases)). Bottom left, where Skate 3 has it: the trick's name with CLEAN or SKETCHY, the line's timer as a ring with the multiplier inside and your stance beside it (or SWITCH and FAKIE, as Skate 3 labels a trick), and the trick's score over the line's. Session markers say what they did: **Session Marker Placed**; holding to return fills a bar under **Return to Marker Position**; a press too short to return shows **HOLD**. The words are Skate 3's own, from your setup. While you skate, the game's action buttons in that corner are hidden, and V's figure (bottom right) turns into a skateboard while she rides. Set `enabled=0` under `[hud]` in `deckrunner.ini` to turn it off. Without Codeware nothing changes.
- **The skateboard in the weapon wheel** (optional: needs [TweakXL](https://github.com/psiberx/cp2077-tweak-xl/releases) and [ArchiveXL](https://github.com/psiberx/cp2077-archive-xl/releases)). V gets a **Skateboard**: equip it in a weapon slot once (Inventory, Weapons), then pick it in the weapon wheel to start skating. On foot, hold **Y** for the wheel: pick the Skateboard to stop, or a weapon to stop and draw it; close the wheel (B) to keep skating. Without TweakXL and ArchiveXL nothing changes and Ctrl+Shift+F10 works as before.

## Downloads

- **Deckrunner-0.2.0.zip**: the plugin. Install it like any mod: Vortex, MO2, or extract it into the game folder.
- The setup hasn't changed: **Deckrunner-Setup-1.zip** is attached to the [0.1.3 release](https://github.com/londonbrown/Deckrunner/releases/tag/v0.1.3).

## Upgrading from 0.1.3 or 0.1.2

Install **Deckrunner-0.2.0.zip** over your existing install (or with your mod manager). That's all: your data, archives and `deckrunner.ini` stay as they are, and you don't need to run setup again. The new settings use their defaults until you add them; `deckrunner.default.ini` beside your `deckrunner.ini` lists them all.

## New install

1. Install [RED4ext](https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/2380).
2. Install **Deckrunner-0.2.0.zip** (Vortex, MO2, or extract into the game folder).
3. Download **Deckrunner-Setup-1.zip** from the [0.1.3 release](https://github.com/londonbrown/Deckrunner/releases/tag/v0.1.3), extract it anywhere outside the game folder and run `Deckrunner Setup.cmd`. It finds Cyberpunk 2077 (Steam or GOG) and asks for your Skate 3 disc image (`.iso`) or extracted folder. It takes a few minutes; a rerun picks up where it stopped.
4. In game: load a save and press **Ctrl+Shift+F10** to skate (or pick the Skateboard in the weapon wheel). Press **Y** to get on the board.

## Requirements

- Cyberpunk 2077 2.5 (PC, Steam or GOG)
- [RED4ext](https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/2380)
- Your own Skate 3 Xbox 360 disc, as an `.iso` or extracted folder (tested: title ID 454108E6, media ID 5C087C2C, no title update)
- A gamepad (Xbox controllers, or a PS5 DualSense without Steam Input)
- About 3 GB free during setup (about 1.6 GB stays installed)
- Optional, for the weapon wheel: TweakXL and ArchiveXL
- Optional, for the HUD: Codeware (and redscript)

Tested with female V; male V should work but hasn't been tested yet.
